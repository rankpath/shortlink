2. Logo

The logo is the word "ShortLink" in heavy deep purple, with four pink brackets in the "o" position. The brackets are a scan frame: it is the one visual idea in the brand. The app icon is the same frame in pale pink on a pink square.

Primary. Full colour on white. Use at 120 px wide or more.
On blush or lavender. Full colour works on both.
On deep purple. Use the white logo. The brackets stay pink.
App icon and favicon. Pink square with pale brackets. Minimum 24 px.

Rules

  • Clear space. Keep at least the height of the letter "S" free on every side.
  • Minimum size. Wordmark 120 px wide on screen. Icon 24 px.
  • App header. The wordmark is 30 px tall (about 168 px wide).
  • Write the name as "Short Link" in running text, in the same font as the text around it.
Don't
  • Recolour the brackets, or make only part of the wordmark a different colour.
  • Put the full-colour logo on pink, on photos, or on deep purple (use the white logo there).
  • Add shadows, outlines, glows or gradients. Stretch or skew the logo.
  • Use the pale bracket colour alone as an icon on white. It is too pale.

Colour pattern. Mostly white and blush. Navy Ink carries the words. Purple marks links and icons. Pink marks the one main action and the data. Deep Purple is for the logo and the dark API section. The WCAG AA rule: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and icons. Values are measured with the WCAG 2.1 formula from the hex codes above.

Colour rules

  • One pink primary button per screen, with Navy Ink text. Other actions use the ghost button (white, navy text, thin border).
  • Never put white text on pink. Never use pink for body text or small text on white.
  • QR codes use Deep Purple, Purple or black. Pink is too light for reliable scanning.
  • Keep this purple palette apart from the Mergepdftool blue palette.

7. Decisions and open items

  • Credit line. Decided: "by Mergepdftool", as plain text, with no link yet. Add a URL later if you want one.
  • Vector logo files. The logo and icon files are cut from the PNG brand sheets (wordmark 626 px wide, icon 512 px). Print and very large sizes need SVG or AI files.
  • Hex codes on the palette sheet. The sheet prints Primary as #FB6F92 (pink), but its swatch and the logo are deep purple, so this guide uses the swatch colour #2D0B5B. The sheet prints Purple as #7C3AED while the swatch measures #8543F4. This guide uses the printed #7C3AED, which passes 5.70:1. Please confirm both.
  • Tagline. "Short links. Smart QR." comes from the brand sheet. It is not in the app yet, and it has no Thai version.
  • Name check. "Short Link" is a generic phrase. Check it can be registered in the markets you sell in before printing it on materials.
  • Status pages. The not-found, disabled and expired pages are English only. Thai copy for them still needs writing; section 6 only covers the list labels.

Contrast is measured with the WCAG 2.1 formula from the hex values. Logo colours are sampled from the brand sheet's own pixels, so values can differ by one or two points between files. Audience and business notes are not in this guide.
