2. Logo

The logo is the wordmark with four brackets in the "o" position. The brackets are a scan frame: it is the one visual idea in the brand.

Primary. Signal Pink on white. Use at 120 px wide or more.
One colour, Navy. Use below 120 px wide, in the app header, and on blush.
On blush. Navy only. The pink logo never goes on blush.
Symbol. The four brackets, on Navy or Signal Pink only. For small icons on white, use the app icon.
App icon and favicon. Pink square with pale brackets.

Rules

  • Clear space. Keep at least the height of the letter "h" free on every side.
  • Minimum size. Pink wordmark 120 px wide. Navy wordmark 96 px wide. Symbol 24 px.
  • Write the name as "Short Link" in running text, in the same font as the text around it.
Don't
  • Recolour the brackets, or make only part of the wordmark a different colour.
  • Put the pink logo on Deep Rose, on blush, on photos, or on navy.
  • Add shadows, outlines, glows or gradients. Stretch or skew the logo.
  • Use the bracket tint alone as an icon on white. It is too pale (see contrast below).

Colour pattern. Mostly white and blush. Navy carries the words. Deep Rose marks what you can click. Signal Pink is the signature, used sparingly. The WCAG AA rule: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and icons. Values are measured from the logo files and the hex codes above.

Colour rules

  • One Deep Rose primary button per screen. Other actions use the ghost button (white, navy text, thin border).
  • Use Signal Pink for the logo and large accents. Never for body text or small icons on white.
  • Keep Navy and the Mergepdftool navy apart. The Short Link navy is close to it, so let pink, not navy, carry the identity.

7. Decisions and open items

  • Credit line. Decided: "by Mergepdftool", as plain text, with no link yet. Add a URL later if you want one.
  • Vector logo files. The logo pack is PNG only. Print and very large sizes need SVG or AI files. Please send them, or confirm the PNG pack is final.
  • Name check. "Short Link" is a generic phrase. Check it can be registered in the markets you sell in before printing it on materials.
  • Status pages. The not-found, disabled and expired pages are English only. Thai copy for them still needs writing; section 6 only covers the list labels.

Contrast is measured with the WCAG 2.1 formula from the hex values. Colour shares follow the Mergepdftool pattern. Logo colours are sampled from the pack's own pixels, so values can differ by one or two points between files. Audience and business notes are not in this guide.
